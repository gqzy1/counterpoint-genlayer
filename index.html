THE REASONING METHOD

Follow the tension.

An arrow means an accepted attack from one argument to another. The argument at the arrowhead is being challenged.

  1. An argument with no undefeated attackers can stand (IN).
  2. An argument attacked by a standing argument is defeated (OUT).
  3. Repeat until nothing changes. Remaining arguments stay open (UNDECIDED).

Grounded semantics, after P. M. Dung. The contract interprets only the signed arguments and proposed links. It does not verify external facts, infer missing links, or establish overall truth. The accepted leader’s explanatory wording is shown as context; validators must agree on every challenge label.
